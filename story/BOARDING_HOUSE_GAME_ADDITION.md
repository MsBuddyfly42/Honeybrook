# Welcome House boarding-house expansion — author reminder

This records Nell's requested game design for later manuscript adaptation. It does not rewrite Book One.

Welcome House is a huge timber-and-stone cottage/cabin boarding house with three storeys, connected wings, domestic windows, covered porch and accessible entrance. Visitors arrive outside and choose to enter the front hall. A receptionist works at an ordinary household writing desk with a guest register and keys.

Rooms: Front hall and reception desk; Boarders’ sitting room; Boarding-house kitchen; Shared dining room; Study and planning room; Cozy shared sleeping room · 20 bunk areas; Quiet guest bedroom; Private washroom; Laundry and linen room; Attic keepsake room.

The shared sleeping space is **one big wide cozy bedroom**, with twenty bunk areas grouped around the room, two beds per area and forty shared sleeping places. Quilts, curtains, low shelving, rugs, storage and a central family sitting nook make it part of a house. The owner explicitly rejected a long corridor or reform-school/barracks layout. The separate quiet guest bedroom preserves Nell's established private stay.

Ground-floor family rooms and the broad bedroom connect through the front hall; house stairs lead to the quiet guest room and attic. There are ten local tasks per room with actual preparation, work, inspection and handover. Bunk-area selectors inspect the layout; they do not invent occupancy records.

The original game music is now Honeybrook Morning, a bright D-major woodland two-step at 112 BPM with plucked melody, chord rhythm, bass and soft brush percussion. The Fair score uses a livelier 104–116 BPM.

The original manuscript, source chapter photographs, Harold's letters, histories, balances and previous save keys remain unchanged. These game additions await the author's later manuscript revision.

## Whole families are welcome

The author added **ten private family sleeping quarters**, so parents/caregivers and cubs can remain together when a household needs help. Each room has adult sleeping space, cub beds, family storage and a home-like sitting/reading nook; some show an infant-cot corner or wider access. Rooms 1–2 are on the ground floor, 3–7 on the first upper floor and 8–10 on the second upper floor. The separate wide shared bedroom remains available as well.

A shared bathroom quarter provides six private toilet stalls and six handwashing sinks. A separate private accessible family washroom has supported-access space and a changing table. Laundry/linen facilities, supervised cub play, private family support and clothing/bear necessities complement shared meals and comfortable sitting areas. Welcome House is Honeybrook's first introduction: a welcoming porch, a patient home-style reception, a place to rest and help suited to what a visitor or family asks for. These are game additions for later manuscript revision, not changes to Book One.
