Where the Stories Live · Book One · Templar Hughes-Bryant.

The original manuscript was supplied by its author for this game. All 69 chapters, the prologue and epilogue are preserved in full. Download the unchanged original Word document or read the complete source text.

Photographic-style scenes

Twenty original generated scene sheets contain 80 views: 71 chapter scenes, a town cover and eight purpose-specific room views. Four portrait sheets depict 24 original characters. These are imaginative photographic-style interpretations of a fictional bear world, not documentary photographs. Unspecified clothing, appearance and furnishings are design choices, not additional manuscript facts.

Scenes were reviewed against the source for species, historical chronology, family membership, supported participation, the distinct bridges and unresolved mysteries. The Guardian Goddess has no invented bodily portrait. Historical scenes are memories. Character-directory notes distinguish historical and current characters.

Interactive observation, arranging, packing and inspection practices are game adaptations. They preserve the author's outcomes, do not replace the complete prose and do not award money, admission or spiritual standing.
