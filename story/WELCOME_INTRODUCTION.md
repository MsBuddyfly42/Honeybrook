# Honeybrook’s photographic entrance introduction

Game-adaptation welcome dialogue for the author’s later reference. The source manuscript is unchanged. This introductory tour does not advance Nell’s research or start her first conversation with Harold.

1. **Sammy Patchwell · Welcome House.** “Come and sit for a moment. You don’t have to know your way around yet. We can show you.” The image shows Sammy in his patched coat and a welcoming bear beside Welcome House and the wooden creek bridge.
2. **Amelia Honeybrook · Town Square.** “A meal, a chair, and time to find your feet. Start there. Our square has room for a quiet visitor as well as a celebration.” The town square photograph accompanies Amelia’s original bear cutout.
3. **Wally Crumbwell · Main Street.** “Follow the smell of baking to Cub Cakes & Crumbs. You can watch, learn, or simply enjoy something warm. There are BEARnecessities—and treats, too.” The Main Street photograph accompanies Wally’s original apron-wearing bear cutout.
4. **The neighborhood paths · Bridgeview.** “A familiar porch, a garden gate, a bridge into Bear Hollow. There are neighbors to meet, bees to learn about, and places to return to when they begin to feel like home.” Bridgeview’s photograph and Sammy’s original cutout introduce the connected paths without resolving any family mystery.
5. **A little farther along · Honeybrook Fair.** “And when you’re ready for a BEARtastic afternoon, follow the music: rides, games, food stands and the little circus. The Fair has its own entrance. Honeybrook will be here when you return.” The existing bear-family midway photograph preserves the separate Fair portal.

Visitors can pause, browse scenes, hear optional narration or enter at any point. The new front page retains Honeybrook’s name and motto, the original bear characters and the approved photographic country setting. The legacy welcome-card controls remain hidden for old scripts to use internally; they are not a second visible entrance.
