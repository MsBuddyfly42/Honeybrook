## Confirmed identity and purpose

- Nell is the player's self-inspired adult human protagonist, age **42**, **married**. Do not import the real family's names; those relatives already have separate authored roles in Honeybrook.
- Nell is the first **present-day** human arrival. Historical Goldilocks and her human beekeeper parents remain in their proper past scenes.
- Nell comes to learn about Honeybrook, the place she heard about throughout childhood, and to follow her family's missing-beekeeper history. Wealth is not her reason for visiting, a condition of welcome, a score of worth, or a resolution of the Northern Woods mystery.
- Her late grandfather left her an **$800 trillion** inheritance and told her about the missing relatives who were beekeepers: Goldilocks and her family.
- Goldilocks's mother was the daughter of the owner of oil-rich land. The family's wealth came from that land and oil; the author's “princess, so to speak” comparison describes her privileged background, not a new literal royal title.
- Nell's grandfather was the **grandson of Goldilocks's mother’s brother**. Nell and Goldilocks are related along that family branch. Goldilocks’s maternal uncle and unnamed intermediary relatives are author-supplied additions, not names recovered from the original manuscript.
- The corrected branch is: the oil-land owner → Goldilocks’s mother’s brother → his child → Nell’s grandfather → Nell’s parent → Nell. The same oil-land owner → Goldilocks’s mother → Goldilocks. This aligns with the author’s original great-great-great-grandfather description for the landowner. No additional family names, marriages or dates are supplied.
