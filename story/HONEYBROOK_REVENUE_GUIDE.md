# Honeybrook revenue services — operating guide

These are new optional game services and fictional dollar prices. Original manuscript history, existing businesses, personal wallets and property ownership remain unchanged. No real payments are collected.

## Start and progression

Open `enterprises.html` to inspect ten photographic desks and all 44 offers. Nell’s operating role unlocks only after her family discovery and her deliberate choice to stay. Three completed standard orders at an enterprise unlock its premium offers. There are three completed or reserved orders per enterprise per community day, and at most ten simultaneous unfinished orders.

The operator completes eight saved actions per order: Check the customer and supplies; Arrange the work plan; Choose the quality standard; Perform action one; Perform action two; Perform action three; Check the invoice; Hand over and post the receipt. A reserved customer payment remains held for fulfilment; only the final checked handover posts earned revenue. Wrong checks do not charge or advance. A completed order cannot pay twice.

## Money and ownership

Town counters and rentals retain the sale less supplier, staff and preparation costs. Independent co-op instructors, guides and couriers retain their proceeds after those costs and a disclosed fixed town fee. A market stall generates only its rent in the city ledger; the vendor’s own retail sales are separate. The existing Fair account remains separate: $2 admission tickets, two tickets per ride or a $30 one-Fair-day ride wristband; games and concessions separate; prize tickets stay earned. Town Hall combines retained funds for review without writing either ledger into the other.

## Bookings, cancellation and saved work

Designated guest units and campsites are reserved for the requested nights. Existing privately owned cottages and Nell’s room are excluded. A Fair event space, each numbered market stall and Harmony Hall has one exclusive daily session. Rescheduling a paused booking preserves its completed work and checks the new dates. An unstarted order may be cancelled and its held payment refunded; started work is kept for completion. Beginning another community day preserves unfinished orders and all existing cases, rooms and promises; it does not reset older town/camp projects. Backups, checked restore previews, checkpoints and stale-tab protection cover the new enterprise record. No automatic historical sales, personal profit transfers, public-project spending or donations are invented.

## Weather and essential access

Essential refuge, care, ordinary welcome, public paths and belonging remain free. Paid trading work pauses during a simulated storm. The global warning retains its 30-second shelter route prompt. The fictional storm ends after three minutes of real elapsed time, clears the red banner and siren, and displays an eight-second all-clear. Its clock survives navigation and reload. Original shelter check-ins and unfinished work are retained; quiet town-refuge readiness practice can continue afterward without restarting the alarm. Repeated camp warnings have unique incident identifiers. Manual full-readiness all-clear remains available sooner when its actual checks are complete. This is game weather, not real emergency guidance.
