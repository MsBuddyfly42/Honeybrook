## Committed story mode

Starting the journey commits this browser save to story mode. There is no button back to unrestricted town or Fair play. Closing the game pauses the story; returning resumes the saved action. Previously introduced opening saves enter this mode when the player returns to My Journey. Older town/Fair finances, possessions and activities are preserved.

Travel is a dedicated photographic story visit to the place needed for the current inquiry, case or assignment. Direct free-play addresses, Back navigation and additional same-browser tabs return to the journey. The emergency refuge remains accessible during an active community alert and returns to the story after all-clear.

## The Guardian Goddess and HINT

The author confirmed that Nell’s dream voice is the same Guardian Goddess who watches over and protects Honeybrook and Bear Hollow. She offers episode guidance without naming an embodied appearance or resolving the older forest mysteries. All new guidance dialogue is a game addition for the author’s later manuscript revision.

HINT stays in the story bar. It offers a gentle nudge, a clearer direction, then an explained next step or supported answer. Help follows the current opening observation, research question, case check/conclusion, room project, seasonal practice, work assignment, delivery, visit or future scene. Asking never performs an action, completes a task, consumes an item or advances the story. The dialog supports keyboard dismissal and returns focus to HINT.

## Activity planning corrections

- Every case check now names the object or record and places it where Nell can reasonably inspect it. The humming book, flower and reading-room window are all checked at the library; the handcart and its wheel stay at the bridge. A case need not require three unrelated districts.
- A witness introduces access to a check. Inspecting supplies its actual finding; clicking “listen” no longer reveals the identical observation twice.
- All forty cases have competing, case-specific explanations with varied answer positions. Evidence supports the conclusion; hints can explain why after the observations are gathered.
- Comparing sources is not treated as a chronology puzzle. Six research boards and all citizen evidence boards accept complete comparisons without arbitrary sorting. Family genealogy and the actual timeline retain meaningful ordering.
- Recovery cases wait for a confirmed all-clear. A solution still requires an agreed repair, return, correction or handover before closure.
- Apiary sessions clearly use seasonal demonstration equipment, records and prepared supplies. Clicking through them does not grow bees or advance the calendar. Equipment is used for the stated inspection, writing, fitting or preparation step rather than always “placed” on a table.

This revision audits the journey, family research and community case/work presentation. It does not claim that every older standalone mini-game has been redesigned.
