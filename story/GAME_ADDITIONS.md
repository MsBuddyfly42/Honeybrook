## Community holiday traditions

### Community Thanksgiving Gathering

Fourth Thursday in November · Town Square.

Thanksgiving in Honeybrook is gratitude to God, remembrance, family, community and care for neighbors. A shared harvest meal is one part of a deeper gathering; everyone has a place at the table.

The community pauses to give thanks to God for life, provision, family and the neighbors who carry one another through difficult seasons. Loved ones are remembered, Honeybrook’s beginnings are honored, and gratitude becomes action: a place at the table, a shared portion and a visit for someone who cannot come. The feast is an expression of that care.

- **Gather in gratitude and remembrance**: Prepare a quiet reflection corner; Welcome neighbors to a voluntary blessing or quiet reflection; Pause to remember loved ones and Honeybrook’s beginnings; Arrange portions and visits for neighbors unable to attend.
- **Set the Town Square tables**: Lay the harvest tablecloths; Place the plates and cups; Leave wide clear aisles and family spaces; Arrange a quiet nearby table.
- **Cook the harvest supper**: Measure the ingredients; Mix the bowl; Bake / simmer the holiday batch; Inspect and label the finished dish.
- **Make the community gratitude tree**: Write a gratitude leaf: blessings, family, remembrance or a neighbor you want to thank..
- **Lead the harvest melody**: Play the community melody at an unhurried pace.
- **Serve every community place**: Welcome each registered community place with a portion or non-food favor.
- **Leave the square welcoming**: Gather reusable tableware; Pack the remaining portions for sharing; Wipe the tables and clear the paths; Return the kitchen and square supplies.

Finale: Open the community Thanksgiving dinner. Keepsake: Harvest table quilt square.

### Christmas Community Jubilee

December · Town Square.

The Christmas Jubilee honors the birth of Jesus Christ alongside Santa Claus traditions, joyful giving, carols and the lighting of the community tree. Sacred remembrance and playful Christmas wonder both belong here.

At the Nativity, Honeybrook remembers Jesus Christ’s birth in Bethlehem: Mary and Joseph, a baby laid in a manger, the shepherds and the good news of hope. The community makes room for prayer, carols and quiet reflection. Alongside that sacred remembrance, a bear neighbor dressed as Santa welcomes families, gathers wish letters and shares handmade gifts. Faith, generosity and childhood wonder fill the same Jubilee.

- **Prepare the Christmas Nativity display**: Stable backdrop; Manger; Mary and Joseph figurines; Baby Jesus figurine; Shepherd figurines; Bethlehem star.
- **Santa’s community gift visit**: Set Santa’s chair beside a wide family path; Prepare the wish-letter basket; Welcome the bear neighbor dressed as Santa; Arrange a handmade gift for each family.

## Whole-community storm refuge

The refuge is at Town Square and serves all thirteen districts, Bear Hollow, Fair guests and the active camp roster. Its main wing has 400 places, with a separately opened 400-place reserve. Families are checked in and assigned together; Lena participates with her family through supported seating, transfer and quiet-space preparation. Visiting parties can be registered. Staff route reports, stock, water, meals, first aid, lights, radio, backup power, sanitation, bench repair and staff reports must be ready before all-clear. A turnover sequence prepares the refuge for the next gathering. The original camp shelter remains a separate place.

- **Reception & family check-in** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.
- **Family sleeping wing** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.

- **First-aid & care room** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.
- **Community kitchen & water station** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.
- **Accessible bathrooms & sanitation** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.
- **Backup power & communications** — Connected refuge room; see its hands-on activities in the game.

The town and camp storm scenarios trigger a loud siren, a persistent red alert and a thirty-second warning clock. A quickest connected district route leads to shelter. The warning continues after the clock expires and does not erase progress. The siren can be silenced separately from the visible alert. These are fictional game systems.

## Day, night and restrained lighting

Photographic Day, Night and Follow game time views cover the connected world. Exterior scenes use moonlight; interiors use warm light. A small fixture at the edge of relevant scenes gives a modest warm pool of light, with open darkness around it. Holiday scenes retain only a few practical lights; Christmas tree lights and a few Halloween pumpkin lanterns remain focal points. There are no floodlights or flashing decorative effects.

## Fair and circus programs

The separate Fair portal includes these 47 expanded programs, with preparation, hands-on work, inspection and delivery. Progression has three levels, prizes and keepsakes; a program can be completed up to three times per camp day.

- **Ribbon dance**: Clear the ring; Mark the travel lanes; Rehearse the ribbon path; Bow with the ensemble. Keepsake: Ribbon rosette.
- **Beanbag juggling**: Check the soft bags; Start below shoulder height; Echo the toss sequence; Catch and collect the bags. Keepsake: Juggler’s beanbag patch.
- **Low balance trail**: Ask the adult spotter; Place the floor-level board; Trace the balance route; Step to the clear finish. Keepsake: Balance trail pennant.
- **Kind clown routine**: Ask before inviting a volunteer; Show the empty flowerpot; Reveal the fabric blossom; Thank the volunteer without teasing. Keepsake: Fabric blossom keepsake.
- **Puppet theater**: Choose the welcome story; Check sightlines and quiet seating; Rehearse entrances; Close with the whole cast. Keepsake: Puppet playbill.
- **Clockwork bee choreography**: Disconnect the practice model; Mark safe turns; Guide it around the route; Park it on its stand. Keepsake: Golden bee route card.
- **Ensemble circus finale**: Keep the exit lane open; Invite seated or standing participation; Follow the ensemble cues; Bring every performer into the bow. Keepsake: Ensemble finale ribbon.
- **Build the low stage**: Measure the floor plan; Fit the model supports; Secure the four model pegs; Ask Tom to inspect before opening. Keepsake: Stage crew badge.
- **Inspect model rigging**: Keep the model unpowered; Inspect each attachment; Tag the damaged sample; Record a crew-only repair. Keepsake: Rigging inspection card.
- **Run the lighting desk**: Cover the cable route; Set a comfortable light level; Follow the color score; Fade the lights gently. Keepsake: Lighting cue sheet.
- **Mix the welcome sound**: Lower the master before connecting; Set voice above music; Check the quiet listening option; Save the comfortable mix. Keepsake: Sound crew patch.
- **Repair costume trim**: Choose a matching patch; Pin the trim flat; Stitch the border pattern; Check for loose ends. Keepsake: Costume fabric swatch.
- **Seat the audience**: Keep the aisle clear; Offer companion seating; Guide the marked route; Leave a quiet exit available. Keepsake: Usher’s welcome card.
- **Pack the fair supply cart**: Read all three crew requests; Count supplies into bins; Secure the cart load; Check the receiving crews. Keepsake: Crew supply stamp.
- **Honeywheel loading crew**: Wait for a complete stop; Load balanced model cabins; Close each model gate; Check with the adult operator. Keepsake: Honeywheel crew badge.
- **Carousel opening inspection**: Keep the model ride stopped; Check each handhold; Check gates and clear floor; Ask the adult operator to approve. Keepsake: Carousel crew ribbon.
- **Honeywheel landmark trail**: Study the public skyline; Locate the creek bridge; Trace the marked landmarks; Record the return to the gate. Keepsake: Skyline postcard.
- **Carousel music cues**: Offer the stationary bench option; Check comfortable volume; Echo the carousel tune; Finish with a gentle cadence. Keepsake: Carousel music card.
- **Miniature railway dispatcher**: Stop at the station; Check the accessible platform; Send the model along the clear route; Stop before unloading. Keepsake: Railway conductor patch.
- **Ride maintenance workshop**: Isolate the tabletop model; Compare three bearing samples; Rank service readiness; Leave the damaged part tagged. Keepsake: Maintenance notebook.
- **Honey jar ring toss**: Set the jars behind the line; Choose a reachable position; Aim at the marked jar; Collect all rings safely. Keepsake: Ring-toss rosette.
- **Duck pond matching**: Read the symbol key; Lift three model ducks; Rank their matching marks; Return every duck to the tray. Keepsake: Duck pond postcard.
- **Beanbag basket toss**: Set the baskets in a clear lane; Choose seated or standing play; Aim for the marked basket; Count the landed bags. Keepsake: Basket toss patch.
- **Soft ball bottle challenge**: Stack the lightweight bottles; Clear the retrieval lane; Aim at the center stack; Reset the bottles for the next player. Keepsake: Bottle challenge ribbon.
- **Balloon target board**: Inspect the reusable board; Choose the marked balloon target; Aim the soft Velcro ball; Collect it after the lane is clear. Keepsake: Balloon star keepsake.
- **Ladder ball tabletop**: Check the soft practice props; Mark a clear throw lane; Aim for the middle rung; Record the rung score. Keepsake: Ladder ball pennant.
- **Midway scorekeeper**: Read each signed round slip; Add each player’s points; Check tied results fairly; Post the verified scores. Keepsake: Fair-play score card.
- **Vegetable exhibit judging**: Keep grower names covered; Compare soundness and uniformity; Rank the three exhibits; Write the rubric result. Keepsake: Garden judging ribbon.
- **Orchard fruit judging**: Use the exhibit samples only; Check ripeness and bruising; Rank condition before size; Return each entry tag. Keepsake: Orchard exhibit card.
- **Flower arrangement**: Count the reusable stems; Place taller stems behind; Build the balanced arrangement; Check the vase is stable. Keepsake: Pressed-flower bookmark.
- **Honey exhibit grading**: Keep exhibit jars sealed; Compare labels and clarity; Rank the documented entries; Keep tasting separate from grading. Keepsake: Honey judging seal.
- **Livestock care station**: Read the adult steward’s care card; Measure the model feed portions; Check water and shaded rest; Record care without handling an animal. Keepsake: Animal care patch.
- **Seed tray workshop**: Match the seed label; Fill the marked tray cells; Water only the planted cells; Add a readable plant marker. Keepsake: Seedling care card.
- **Harvest basket competition**: Read the three family orders; Count produce by basket; Check the total portions; Deliver the balanced baskets. Keepsake: Harvest basket rosette.
- **Community quilt square**: Choose the shared colors; Lay out the quilt block; Join the marked border; Check the backed seams. Keepsake: Quilt square.
- **Ribbon loom workshop**: Set the warp strips; Weave over and under; Follow the marked repeat; Secure the woven ends. Keepsake: Woven ribbon.
- **Clay bowl studio**: Wedge the soft practice clay; Press a stable flat base; Build and smooth the bowl wall; Leave it labeled for adult firing. Keepsake: Glazed bowl claim card.
- **Candy apple kitchen**: Wash and dry the apples; Let the adult handle hot syrup; Set the cooled model apples; Label the finished portions. Keepsake: Candy apple recipe card.
- **Popcorn kitchen**: Read the six-carton recipe; Measure the practice ingredients; Let the adult manage the hot kettle; Divide and label the servings. Keepsake: Popcorn portion card.
- **Funnel cake kitchen**: Measure the practice batter; Trace the batter on the cool board; Let the adult manage the fryer; Dust and plate cooled portions. Keepsake: Funnel cake recipe card.
- **Corn dog kitchen**: Count sticks and model portions; Measure practice coating; Let the adult manage the fryer; Label six cooled servings. Keepsake: Corn dog recipe card.
- **Community choir**: Offer singing or quiet listening; Check comfortable seating; Echo the rehearsal phrase; Finish together without rushing. Keepsake: Choir keepsake score.
- **Bandstand rhythm**: Keep sound comfortable; Read the four-symbol score; Echo the rhythm; Rest together on the final cue. Keepsake: Bandstand rhythm ribbon.
- **Family dance trail**: Offer seated hand motions too; Clear the shared floor; Follow the marked dance route; Finish with space for everyone. Keepsake: Family dance pennant.
- **Community bake-off judging**: Use the sealed exhibit samples; Check truthful labels first; Rank texture and safe presentation; Record the rubric without buying votes. Keepsake: Bake-off judging ribbon.
- **Cooperative parcel relay**: Offer walking or tabletop roles; Mark the clear handoff route; Carry the parcel through each station; Finish only when every station is included. Keepsake: Cooperative team rosette.
- **Whole-community showcase**: Invite every role and a quiet option; Check clear paths and companion places; Share the completed projects and songs; Thank the community and keep the records. Keepsake: Community showcase playbill.
