# Reception jobs and handwritten ledgers — author reminder

The same desk layout and receptionist work apply to Welcome House, the community storm refuge and the camp storm shelter. Each facility keeps its own guest and accommodation records; all share one housing/construction board so a home cannot be double assigned. Welcome House has ten family rooms, ten special quiet guest rooms and twenty two-bed bunk areas; Nell's original private guest room is excluded. Shelter allocations are game bays/comfort spaces inside their existing accommodation, bounded by 400 community-shelter and 120 camp-shelter places. These do not replace emergency headcounts or issue an all-clear.

The owner is explicit: ALL new ledgers begin EMPTY. No names, facts, entries or default capacities are filled in. Nell types the information herself, without deadlines. A searchable information book supplies complete invented profiles for thirty new game arrivals and source-grounded known details of other bears. Original-book gaps are not silently made manuscript facts.

Separate Individual and Household ledgers contain Name, Story, Room #, Bunk #, household count, household member names/birthdays/ages, height, weight, fur color and eye color. Individuals have one set of personal fields and a place for the player to write count 1. The story section has origin, birthplace, parents, old address, reason for leaving, why Honeybrook and plans. New visitor profiles are game additions for later manuscript revision.

Families stay together; cubs arriving alone can receive quiet supported spaces; adult newcomers who request company can receive shared bunks. Belonging, food and a bed are never earned through payment, usefulness or disclosure. Caregivers respect privacy. The job is regular free play and a highlighted branch of Nell's journey after she is offered her own room, without requiring Harold's meeting or advancing his gates.

Construction Ledger: names/types/numbers planned and progress through planning, building, inspection and completion. Housing notices tell Nell first about variable programmes/batches of 5, 10, 15 or 20 homes. Notices are references only and do not populate ledgers. Available Homes records vacant inspected cottages/cabins and practical requirements. Placement records compare household size, access and any checked role-specific skills, preserve the guest's agreement and require ongoing adult support for lone cubs. No deadline, daily check-in cap, forced checkout or automatic eviction. Reception logging does not itself construct or purchase town buildings.

Preserve the original manuscript bytes, historical characters, authored source histories, all original save IDs and the separate Fair portal. Current live publication must remain on a tested version.
