# Honeybrook community holiday photographs

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, October 23, 2026, for this Git-backed project. All nine final 1536 × 1024 scenes were visually reviewed and converted from PNG to WebP at quality 88. `manifest.json` records the exact final bytes. The original manuscript and earlier artwork were not replaced.

## Prompt set and edits

Shared generation direction: photorealistic natural woodland bear-country scene, lifelike anthropomorphic bears in country clothes, stone and timber architecture, pine/honey palette, realistic textures, accessible wide paths, no humans, lettering, logos or watermark. Unnamed neighbors, not portraits asserting named manuscript identities.

- `thanksgiving.webp`: Town Square harvest gathering at dusk, family tables, harvest foods, gratitude and community hospitality.
- `christmas.webp`: blue-hour Christmas Jubilee, central tree, caroling bears and handmade gifts. Edited central gift-giving adult into a bear neighbor in a traditional Santa costume and added a miniature wooden Nativity with Mary, Joseph and baby Jesus. The carved figures are displayed ornaments, not living humans.
- `christmas-unlit.webp`: lighting-only version of the same Christmas scene with the tree’s own bulbs and star dark before the game’s lighting countdown.
- `easter.webp`: spring gathering with decorated eggs, bear families crafting and sharing a picnic, flowers, music and a country chapel.
- `halloween.webp`: cheerful costumes, pumpkin crafts, treats and lantern procession at night, without frightening monsters.
- `square-spring.webp`, `square-summer.webp`, `square-autumn.webp`, `square-winter.webp`: natural late-afternoon seasonal Town Square settings. Community picnic/craft tables and neighbors, no holiday-specific props. These are shared views of the public location used by the other sixteen celebrations, not unrelated stands or twenty claimed separate event portraits.

The first four event images were edited at the owner’s request: remove roughly 75% of extra fixtures, lanterns, candles, bright windows and excessive golden bloom. Retain only modest entrance/table lights and natural darkness. Keep Christmas’s central tree lights and a few Halloween pumpkin lanterns as focal points. Preserve scene composition, bears, architecture and activities. Runtime fixture decorations are excluded from holiday photographs; their existing practical lighting is sufficient.
