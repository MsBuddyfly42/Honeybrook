# Shelter-matched Honeybrook interiors

Owner-approved visual reference: the inside of the Community Storm Refuge, especially `assets/community-refuge/hall.webp`, with the related kitchen, sleep and care scenes. Created with the built-in image-generation tool; final PNGs were converted to WebP at quality 88. No original artwork was overwritten.

Eight four-view photographic-style sheets provide 32 purpose-specific room scenes. Actual furry brown bears inhabit communal rooms; private sanitation/changing and food storage areas are unoccupied. The final home sheet removes excess candles. These are unnamed background neighbors, not portraits or new manuscript cast. The ordinary human-country drafts and earlier rejected revisions are not used.

`manifest.json` records composition briefs, generation/edit filenames, final dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. Shared direction was photographic bear-country timber/stone architecture matching the shelter, bear-scale furnishings, plum/green textiles, no humans and restrained lighting. Quadrants are numbered 0 top-left, 1 top-right, 2 bottom-left, 3 bottom-right. `interior-room-types.js` maps each room purpose to a quadrant. The existing refuge bunk-room image supplies the bunkhouse bedroom override.

The 32 scenes are reused for matching purposes across addresses; they are not 667 individually generated interiors. These visual/game extensions do not assert that every new room was described in the original manuscript.
