# Where the Stories Wake · original game art

Generated for this owner-authorised Honeybrook expansion in November 2026. Photographic-style fictional scenery, not documentary photographs. Original character designs; no film-franchise designs requested. Fairy-tale humans remain humans, animal residents remain their appropriate species. Nell is human; Harold and the Bear Hollow residents are bears.

The renderer uses SVG view boxes to show individual atlas panels without distorting them. Atlases are preserved intact. `manifest.json` identifies every used view and its source image hash. The first atlas has 12 views (4×3); each `homes-*` and `actions-*` atlas has 16 (4×4); the elder atlas has 20 (5×4). There are 128 composed views across eight images. This is not 128 separately photographed or separately generated files.

`atlas.webp` supplies three matching village states and three matching forest states. Restoration follows actual completed retellings, not discoveries, promises, money or repair clicks. The household atlases follow the 41 named tale destinations in Chapters 41–42, in data order. Extra panels provide woodland, arrival and community views. Action atlases follow the same order, then woodland recovery and communal telling. Elder portraits follow the 20 new elders' data order; Harold retains his blue cap and satchel in the first atlas.

The new elder accounts are fictional additions explicitly requested by the author, not recovered testimony from the original uploaded manuscript. Earlier recollections heard from relatives are identified as such. Names and portraits do not replace original cast IDs. The ancient stone bridge's authorship remains unresolved, and the guide's identity is not exposed in public retellings.

Delivery copies use WebP compression at original dimensions. Generated PNG originals remain in the session’s generated-images library. No panel was cropped or rearranged in the stored atlases.
