# Film School rooms

Ten distinct photographic game rooms were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on October 29, 2026. `manifest.json` records panel assignments and hashes. These are new school environments, not replacement manuscript illustrations. Images are cropped from two contact sheets with a five-pixel inset to remove separators.

Prompt set:

1. Photographic 3×2 equal-panel contact sheet, warm Honeybrook timber/cream-stone woodland film school, no people or lettering: screening theater; tripod-camera studio; two-chair rehearsal stage; notebook/typewriter writing study; dual-monitor editing suite; acoustic recording studio. Believable equipment, cinematic light, distinct interiors.
2. Photographic 2×2 equal-panel contact sheet with the same woodland materials, no people or lettering: softbox/reflector lighting stage; lantern/prop/set workshop; small genre screening room; circular-table criticism library. Distinct layouts, clear walkways, photographic surfaces.

Room indexes 0–9 follow that order. The practice bench photographs are still images; exposure is an illustrative brightness model, editing uses still-image source ranges, and sound monitoring uses synthesized tones. None claims to be real camera capture or recorded dialogue.
