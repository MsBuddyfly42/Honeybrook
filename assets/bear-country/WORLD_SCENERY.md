# Whole-game Bear Hollow scenery

46 original generated 1536 × 1024 WebP sheets provide 184 distinct photographic-style views. Each sheet holds four 768 × 512 views; the shared photo helpers select one tile without downloading four separate files. `world-scene-manifest.json` lists every source, view, and final file hash. These are fictional authored bear-country settings, not documentary photographs. Original approved Bear Hollow cottage/neighborhood photos remain in use.

Coverage: all 86 public town destinations, 30 life/camp rooms, 13 district establishing views, native town homes/workshops/woods, separate Fair midway/circus/rides/booths, cottage porch/kitchen/attic rooms, and baking/garden work stages. The atlas remains a labeled geographic map; playable game boards, drawings and editable paintings remain interactive tools.

Local Fraunces and Nunito fonts use the SIL Open Font License; their original license files are in `fonts/`. No remote photo, font, AI chat or paid service is needed at runtime.
