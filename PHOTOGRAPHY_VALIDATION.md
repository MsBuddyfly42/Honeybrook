Same-place aliases intentionally share a photograph. Source and license details accompany the scenes and are listed in assets/scene-photos/credits.html.

Local browser validation at 1440px and 390px covers destination image loading, original navigation, searchable routes, Home/Go Back, keyboard labels, navigation contrast, fixed scene dimensions, room save/reload, cooking photo progression and unchanged separate Life progress. Photographs depict representative real settings, rather than custom architectural renderings. Lighting, season and outdoor weather controls are visual previews; they do not provide live forecasts, change game dates, or implement new weather consequences. Seasonal presentation uses photographic color treatment rather than separately photographed seasonal versions. Exact matching of window directions and every doorway view is not verified.
